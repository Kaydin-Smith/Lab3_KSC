Kiki's Delivery Service

Kiki’s Delivery Service is a coming-of-age fantasy film directed by Hayao Miyazaki and animated by Studio Ghibli. It follows Kiki, a young witch and her journey as she sets out on her own for the first time. Hopeful at first, she makes friends, gets a job, and is able to live above kind bakers. She finds people who care about her and who she cares about, particularly a young boy named Jiro. However, despite Kiki’s best attempts, she eventually finds herself being homesick, uncertain in herself, and scared of new changes. These feelings fester until Kiki’s powers suffer and she is unable to use them. Kiki needs to overcome her uncertainty in order to help herself, her friends, and her new home.

As one of my favourite childhood movies, I came to the realization that I may have related to her more than I anticipated over these past two years. In these last 2 years, I have had the privilege of going to school full-time, working part-time, taking care of my animals, and renting my very first apartment. Although these are accomplishments, my issues, struggles, and shortcomings have never been clearer. Having struggled with mental health, trauma, and consistent support, I relate heavily to the feeling of having lost my ‘magic’, of course, in a much less literal sense than Kiki.

(Kiki's Delivery Service)
